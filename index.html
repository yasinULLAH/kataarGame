⭐ Katar (6×6) & Sholo Guti (37-Pt)

Traditional Pashtun & South Asian leap-and-capture war game. Step along connected lines or leap over an adjacent enemy bead into an empty point beyond it.

⭐ Navakankari, Teen Guti (3v3) & Chhah Guti (6v6)

Drop beads onto intersections, then slide strictly along connected lines to form a 3-in-a-row Katar (Mill). Each mill lets you remove 1 opponent bead!

🐯 Bagh-Chal (5×5) & Aadu Puli Attam (23-Node)

Asymmetric Tigers vs Goats! Player 1 (Goats) drops goats to surround and trap the Tigers. Player 2 (Tigers) leaps over Goats to eat 5 Goats and win.

🦋 Lau Kata Kati & ☸️ Pretwa (19-Node)

9v9 leap-capture classics on a double-triangle butterfly lattice (Lau Kata Kati) or 3 concentric circles with 6 radial spokes (Pretwa).

⚔️ Fanorona (5×5 & 5×9) & 🛡️ Seega (5×5)

Fanorona: Capture contiguous enemy lines by Approach (moving toward them) or Withdrawal (moving away). Seega: Drop 2 beads at a time, then sandwich enemy beads orthogonally!

👑 Dama (8×8 Kings) & ⚫ Gomoku (9×9)

Dama: Advance forward/sideways and promote to Flying/Omnidirectional Kings on the far row! Gomoku: Pure placement — align 5 stones in a row to win.

⏳ Mid-Game Leave / Disconnect Forfeit Rule: If a player leaves, closes the game, or disconnects mid-match, a known return countdown (default 60s) starts automatically. If they do not return before the countdown expires, victory is awarded to the remaining player!
